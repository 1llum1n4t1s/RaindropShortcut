A Chrome extension that lists the bookmarks you saved in Raindrop.io in a compact view with a single click on the toolbar icon.

■ Main features

・Bookmark list in one click
  Click the toolbar icon to see the bookmarks of the selected collection, sorted by title.

・Collection (folder) selection
  In the settings, choose what to show from your Raindrop.io collection tree.
  You can select sub-collections at any depth, and choosing "All" shows all your bookmarks.

・Incremental search
  Type in the search box to narrow the displayed bookmarks by title or domain name.

・Choose how links open
  In the settings, choose whether bookmarks open in a "New tab" or in the "Current tab".

・Fast display (local cache)
  The bookmark list of the selected collection is fetched in the background every 5 minutes and stored on your device.
  Click the icon to see the saved list right away. If more than 5 minutes have passed since the last fetch, the latest list is fetched while the saved one stays on screen.

・Dark mode support
  Choose the theme from "Auto (follows your OS setting)", "Light" or "Dark".

・Compact layout
  Each bookmark takes one line with its favicon, title and domain name, so even a large number of bookmarks is easy to scan.

・Keyboard operation
  You can also select bookmarks and collections with the Enter / Space keys.

・OAuth 2.0 authentication
  You log in on the Raindrop.io authentication page to connect. You never enter your password in the extension.
  When you log out, the saved login information and the bookmark list cache are erased from your device.

・Contact
  From the bottom of the settings screen, you can send an inquiry about this extension. The first time, you need to verify your identity with a confirmation code sent by email.

■ How to use

1. Install from the Chrome Web Store
2. Click the toolbar icon, then press the "Log in with Raindrop.io" button
3. Authenticate with your Raindrop.io account (first time only)
4. Your bookmark list appears
5. In the settings (⚙ icon), you can change the collection to show, how links open and the theme
